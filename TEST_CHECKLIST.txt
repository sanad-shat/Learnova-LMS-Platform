EDUSPHERE LMS — TEST CHECKLIST

1. Admin
- Add/edit/delete a student
- Add/edit/delete an instructor
- Add/edit/delete a course
- Enroll a student in a course
- Open reports and certificates

2. Instructor
- Log in
- Add a unit and upload a material file
- Add an assignment, quiz, and exam
- Open Submissions and grade student work
- Open Discussions and create/reply to a topic

3. Student
- Log in and open an enrolled course
- Download a unit file and mark the unit complete
- Submit an assignment before its deadline
- Complete a quiz (only one attempt)
- Review notifications, grades, and discussion replies
- Complete every activity and reach 60% or higher
- Generate and print/save the certificate as PDF

4. Persistence
- Add a record, stop the terminal, start again, and confirm it remains.
- Keep instance\ecms.db and uploads\ when moving the project.
