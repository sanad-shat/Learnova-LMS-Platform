Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock a course certificate?

Earn a final grade of at least 60% in the course, then open the Certificates page to view or print your credential.

Can I edit an assignment after submitting it?

You can update your submission before the assignment deadline passes from the course or assignment page.

How do I ask a question about a course or lecture?

Open Discussions from the sidebar, choose New Topic, select your course or Question category, and post your inquiry.
